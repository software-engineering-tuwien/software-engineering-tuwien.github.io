Team and Overview

In this course you carry out a medium-sized software development project in a team, consistently applying an agile development process. The course is divided into two phases: 1.) Bootcamp, and 2.) Group phase.

During the Bootcamp, you will be working on an individual assignment. In this assignment you will develop a web application with Spring Boot and Vue.js. In the group phase you will work in teams of 4-6 students on a software project of your choice where you will be supported by a tutor and will present your progress in regular Management Reviews.

Prerequisites

Mandatory prerequisite is the completion of the introductory and orientation phase (STEOP). We kindly ask for your understanding that we cannot make exceptions here (exchange students excluded).

Furthermore we expect:

  • Object-oriented analysis, design and programming
  • Basics of the Unified Modeling Language (UML)
  • Basic knowledge of algorithms and data structures
  • Basic knowledge of database systems
  • Using a practical programming language and tools (e.g. Java or C++)
  • Applying an integrated development environment and source code management

Course Structure

The course consists of two phases that together amount to 400 points. Both phases must be completed positively for a positive completion of the course.

Bootcamp (total: 100pts)

You work independently during the Bootcamp. The main objective of the Bootcamp is to teach you solid software engineering practices and to prepare you for the group phase.

The Bootcamp consists of:

  • Entry Test (5pts)
  • Individual Assignment (50pts)
  • Live Coding (45pts)
  • Submission Interview

Individual Assignment

During the individual assignment, you will implement a web application using Spring Boot and Vue.js. The assignment will be released on TUWEL after the preliminary discussion and you have about 3 weeks to complete it.

The assignment will be graded by a combination of hidden tests, which will set your base grade, and a submission interview, where a tutor will look at your solution and ask you questions about it. The submission interview can alter your grade in either direction, and it is possible to get a negative grade even if you have passed the hidden tests. The submission interview will take place directly after the live coding.

During the assignment you will have the chance to ask questions and get help from the teaching staff in the TUWEL forum and during tutor hours. Further, we will provide you with intermediate feedback on your progress at set points TBA on TUWEL. The intermediate feedback will consist of a test report generated by the same hidden tests that will be used for final grading. The intermediate feedback will not be graded, but will give you an impression on your progress and how to improve your solution.

It is recommended to start the assignment early and to work on it continuously, as the assignment is designed to be challenging and will require a significant amount of time to complete. Further, starting early ensures that you will get feedback more often.

Live Coding and Submission Interview

Make sure to register in TUWEL for a live coding and interview slot!

During your slot, you will start with a live coding exercise in class. The exercise will be to implement a small feature that extends the individual assignment you've been working on for the last 3 weeks. You will have 45 minutes TODO: 45 minutes final? to complete the exercise. To ensure everyone has the same starting point, we will provide you with a base project that contains a complete implementation of the individual assignment.

After the live coding exercise, you will have a submission interview with a tutor. The tutor will look at your live coding and individual assignment solution and ask you questions about both. The interview will take about 30 minutes TODO: 30 minutes final? and will be used to determine your final grade for the Bootcamp.

The main focus of the interview will be to assess your understanding and ownership of the project. As in real software projects, you are responsible for your solution and should be able to explain your design choices, implementation details and the concepts behind your solution. The tutor will further use the interview to assess your solutions adherence to the technical guidelines and best practices taught in the course.

Group phase (total: 300pts, per group member)

Your team develops a software project using Scrum, supervised by a tutor, and presents progress in three Management Reviews. You will work on a project of either your choice or a predefined project which will be anounce soon in TUWEL.

Group finding

After the live coding and submission interview, everyone who completed the Bootcamp positively will have 3 days to submit group preferences in TUWEL. You can use the group finding forum in TUWEL to find teammates and discuss project ideas. PLEASE NOTE: these are preferences we will try to accommodate, but we cannot guarantee that everyone will get their preferred teammates or project.

Project finding

After we assigned you to your final group, you will have 1 week to submit a project proposal. This proposal can be based on our predefined project or a project of your choice. The proposal should contain a short description of the project, the technologies you will use and the features you will implement, described through user stories.

Technologies

During the group phase, you will be free to choose the technologies you want to use for your project. However, we recommend to use the technologies taught in the Bootcamp (Spring Boot and Vue.js). If you want to choose other technologies, we cannot guarantee support and it would be best to discuss this with your tutor and make sure that your team has the necessary knowledge to use them.

Development

Overall the development phase will last about 10 weeks which will be split into 4 sprints. During this time, you will have weekly meetings with your tutor. The development phase starts after the first Management Review and ends with the final Management Review.

Group Phase Grading

While this is a group project, grading will happen for each individual student and continuously throughout the group phase. Your grade consists of the following components:

  • Sprint reviews (4x 40pts)
  • Technical review (1x 40pts)
  • Overall project success (100pts)

Sprint reviews are made by your tutor. For each sprint your tutor will look at 1-2 merge requests per student and grade them based on the quality of your code, your adherence to the technical guidelines and best practices taught in the course.

The technical review will be conducted by a different tutor than your own. In the week before Management Review 2 another tutor will look at one merge request for each student and grad it based on the same criteria as the sprint reviews.

The overall project success will be assessed by your assistant during the Management Reviews. The assessment will be based on the quality of your final product, the completeness of your project and the overall success of your team.

Overall Grading

The grading consists of 25 % from the Bootcamp and 75 % from the group phase. The Bootcamp consists of an entry test and the solving of an exercise example. The assessment of performance in the group phase takes place within the framework of Management Reviews.

Both phases must be completed positively (≥ 50%) for a positive completion of the course.

Repeatability of partial performances: Since the “Bootcamp” and “group phase” are partial performances that must be completed over a longer period of time (implementation of the individual assignment, development of the group project), there is no possibility of repetition for these partial performances.

The examination mode is continuous assessment (prüfungsimmanent).

Grading scale

All partial and final performances are graded on the same scale from 0 to 100 percent:

  • S1: 88%-100%
  • G2: 75%-87.99%
  • B3: 63%-74.99%
  • G4: 50%-62.99%
  • N5: 0%-49.99%
This results in the following point distribution for your final grade:
  • S1: 352-400pts
  • G2: 300-351.99pts
