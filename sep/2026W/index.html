Team and Overview

In this course you carry out a medium-sized software development project in a team, consistently applying an agile development process. The course is divided into two phases: 1.) Bootcamp, and 2.) Group phase.

During the Bootcamp, you will be working on an individual assignment. In this assignment you will develop a web application with Spring Boot and Vue.js. In the group phase you will work in teams of 4-6 students on a software project of your choice where you will be supported by a tutor and will present your progress in regular Management Reviews.

Learning outcomes

After successful completion of the course, students are able to

  • use the agile software development process Scrum practice-oriented with all aspects;
  • work with modern frameworks for the development of web applications (Spring Boot, Angular);
  • use a distributed source code management system (Git);
  • work with modern software development environments (IntelliJ IDEA);
  • understand and apply modern software architectures;
  • use best practices in a given context;
  • deal successfully with group dynamic and social aspects;
  • present project results in a target-group oriented way.
Subject of course
  • Using a practice-oriented development process model in software development
  • Applying concepts and methods for the individual phases of a software engineering project
  • Selecting and applying concepts, models and tools within a medium-sized software development project
  • Applying techniques for abstraction and modelling in software engineering
  • Creating and adapting high-quality planning and documentation
  • Solving software engineering problems in a distributed team
  • Being responsible for decisions of a role in a project
  • Collecting and maintaining knowledge in a medium-sized team
  • Presenting project results
Teaching methods
  • Accompanying lectures on theoretical concepts and methodological basics
  • Refreshing previous knowledge in tutorials
  • Reflection of one's own previous knowledge on the basis of an exercise example
  • Implementation of a medium-sized software project in a team of 4–6 persons
  • Intensive supervision of the teams by a tutor
  • Intensive use of appropriate software development tools and methods

Prerequisites

Mandatory prerequisite is the completion of the introductory and orientation phase (STEOP). We kindly ask for your understanding that we cannot make exceptions here (exchange students excluded).

Furthermore we expect:

  • Object-oriented analysis, design and programming
  • Basics of the Unified Modeling Language (UML)
  • Basic knowledge of algorithms and data structures
  • Basic knowledge of database systems
  • Using a practical programming language and tools (e.g. Java or C++)
  • Applying an integrated development environment and source code management

Assignments

The course consists of two phases that together amount to 150 hours of work (6 ECTS).

Bootcamp (total: 40 hours)

You work independently: after the preliminary discussion you complete the entry test, familiarise yourself with the technologies used, and implement an individual assignment.

  • Preliminary discussion — 1 hour
  • Visit to tutorials (optional) — 5 hours
  • Entry test — 0.5 hours
  • Familiarisation with the technologies used — 8 hours
  • Implementation individual assignment — 30.5 hours

Group phase (total: 110 hours, per group member)

Your team develops a software project using Scrum, supervised by a tutor, and presents progress in three Management Reviews.

  • Kickoff meeting — 1 hour
  • 10× meeting with tutor — 10 hours
  • 3× Management Review — 3 hours
  • Group-internal meetings, project management — 22 hours
  • Familiarization with the domain — 7 hours
  • Deepening in the applied technologies — 7 hours
  • Implementation — 45 hours
  • Quality assurance / testing — 15 hours

Grading

The grading consists of 25 % from the Bootcamp and 75 % from the group phase. The Bootcamp consists of an entry test and the solving of an exercise example. The assessment of performance in the group phase takes place within the framework of Management Reviews.

Both phases must be completed positively for a positive completion of the course.

Repeatability of partial performances: Since the “Bootcamp” and “group phase” are partial performances that must be completed over a longer period of time (implementation of the individual assignment, development of the group project), there is no possibility of repetition for these partial performances.

The examination mode is continuous assessment (prüfungsimmanent).
