Academic Honesty

We expect all students to do their own work. Where work is done in teams, every team member is expected to contribute substantially and to be able to explain and defend any part of the submission, including parts primarily written by others.

Any kind of plagiarism — including submitting code or text authored by someone else without attribution, sharing solutions between teams, or presenting work from a previous edition of the course as your own — will result in expulsion from the course with a grade N5 (Nicht Genügend).
