AI Usage Policy

You may use AI tools in this course. You remain responsible for every artifact you submit, including code, tests and models. You must understand your work, be able to explain your decisions and take clear ownership of the result, whether or not AI helped produce it.

Submit a written reflection on your AI use for each unit of grading: {% if page.course == "sep" %}one for the Bootcamp and one for each sprint in the Group Phase{% else %}one for each sprint{% endif %}. State whether you used AI, what you used it for, and how you checked and incorporated its output. If you did not use AI, say so in your reflection.

Reflection Prompts

For your 200-word reflection, you may consider the following prompts to guide your writing:

  • How did you evaluate the AI's output?
  • Did you need to correct or refine its suggestions? Why?
  • Did any responses surprise you? Why?
  • Did the AI speed up tasks you could have done yourself? Which?
  • Did the AI provide information or explanations you did not previously have? What?
