AI Usage Policy

AI writing and coding assistants (e.g., ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Codex, Claude, etc.) can be useful tools during the learning process. In this course, you may use such tools unless a specific assignment explicitly states otherwise.

The goal of this course is to build your own technical understanding and problem-solving skills. AI tools must support your learning rather than replace it.

Required AI Usage Submission (PDF)

For every submission, you must hand in an AI Usage document as a PDF—even if you did not use AI tools at all. The document must be submitted as a single PDF file in addition to your solution.

The document must include:

  1. A transcript of your AI interactions (prompts and responses) for any AI system used.
    • If the platform provides a shareable link (e.g., ChatGPT shared links), you may include the link.
    • Otherwise, paste (screenshots of) the transcript directly into the document.
  2. A reflection of approximately 200 words, written without AI assistance, describing your experience.
  3. A statement of AI usage:
    • If you used AI: explain how it contributed to your work (as detailed above).
    • If you did not use AI: explicitly state that no AI tools were used.

How AI Use Will Be Evaluated

If AI tools were used, grading will be a holistic evaluation of your submission, your AI transcripts, and your reflection. Your reflection must justify how the AI contributed to your learning.

Failure to Provide the AI Usage Document

The AI Usage document is mandatory for every submission. Submissions without the required PDF will not be accepted and will receive 0 points.

Reflection Prompts

For your 200-word reflection, you may consider the following prompts to guide your writing:

  • How did you evaluate the AI's output?
  • Did you need to correct or refine its suggestions? Why?
  • Did any responses surprise you? Why?
  • Did the AI speed up tasks you could have done yourself? Which?
  • Did the AI provide information or explanations you did not previously have? What?
