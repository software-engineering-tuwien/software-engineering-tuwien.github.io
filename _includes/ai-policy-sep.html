AI Usage Policy

You may use AI tools in this course. You remain responsible for every artifact you submit, including code, tests and models. You must understand your work, be able to explain your decisions and take clear ownership of the result, whether or not AI helped produce it.

Submit a written reflection on your AI use for each unit of grading: one for the Bootcamp and one for each sprint in the Group Phase. State whether you used AI, what you used it for, and how you checked and incorporated its output. If you did not use AI, say so in your reflection.
